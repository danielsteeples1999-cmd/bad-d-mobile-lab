# Research Pass 04 — DJ Decision Intelligence

Do not implement.

Research how DJ decisions depend on musical structure and context.

Investigate possible states such as:
- establish
- build
- reinforce
- intensify
- tease
- delay
- disrupt
- release
- reset
- re-entry
- contrast
- recover
- extend
- transition
- social/reset window

Treat these as hypotheses, not facts.

Research how professional DJ systems and DJs use:
- BPM
- key/harmony
- phrase structure
- energy
- rhythmic compatibility
- arrangement
- vocals
- breakdowns
- drops
- tension/release
- crowd feedback
- venue/context

Distinguish "musically compatible" from "strategically useful".

Determine which variables can be automated, which need human control, and which require live feedback.

Deliver a cited DJ decision model and experimental roadmap.
Save it in this folder.
