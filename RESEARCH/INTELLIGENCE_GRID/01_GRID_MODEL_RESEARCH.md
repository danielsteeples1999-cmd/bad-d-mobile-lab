# Research Pass 01 — Intelligence Grid Model

Do not code.

Define what a full musical intelligence grid must represent if its purpose is to understand how music controls expectation, movement, tension, release, interruption, and re-entry.

Research:
- beat, downbeat, bar, subdivision
- BPM and tempo changes
- time signature and changing meter
- phrase and section structure
- syncopation, pickups, tuplets
- polymeter and polyrhythm
- structural boundaries
- energy/tension/release
- expectation and prediction error
- transition opportunities
- possible human-response annotations

Determine what is objectively measurable, what is perceptual, what requires human annotation, and what is only a hypothesis.

Do not assume 4/4 is the global truth.

Deliver:
A. definition of the Intelligence Grid
B. layered data model
C. current MIR/DJ approaches
D. information lost by a conventional beat grid
E. vocabulary
F. open research questions
G. cited sources and datasets

Save the completed report in this folder.
