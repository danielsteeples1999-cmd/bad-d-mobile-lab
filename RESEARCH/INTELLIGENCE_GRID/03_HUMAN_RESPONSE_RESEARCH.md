# Research Pass 03 — Musical Structure to Human Response

Do not code and do not claim that crowd behaviour can be directly inferred from audio.

Research the boundary between measurable musical properties and human-response hypotheses.

Investigate:
- groove
- entrainment
- rhythmic prediction
- expectation
- prediction error
- syncopation
- surprise
- tension/release
- perceived energy
- movement synchronisation
- dance response
- audience engagement
- repetition and habituation
- climax and reset

For each concept distinguish:
A. audio fact
B. perceptual interpretation
C. human-response hypothesis
D. observed crowd response

Identify candidate measurable features and experimental designs.

Do not reduce these phenomena to one "danceability" score.

Deliver cited research, datasets, candidate annotations, limitations, and a proposed layered model.
Save the report in this folder.
